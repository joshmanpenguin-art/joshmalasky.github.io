Music Info

Genre

My top genres consist of soundtrack, hard rock, J-pop, nu metal, and OST. Soundtracks and OSTs feature music created to enhance movies, shows, games, and other media, often adding emotion and atmosphere to a scene. Hard rock is known for its heavy guitars, powerful drums, and energetic sound. J-pop is Japanese popular music that often blends catchy melodies with a variety of styles and influences. Nu metal combines heavy metal with elements of hip-hop, alternative rock, and electronic music, creating a distinctive and aggressive sound.

Band

My top artists include Deftones, Toby Fox, Yasunori Mitsuda, Yoko Shimomura, ConcernedApe, Lorien Testard, and Re-Logic. Deftones are known for their atmospheric blend of alternative metal and nu metal, while Toby Fox is recognized for his memorable video game soundtracks, especially in Undertale and Deltarune. Yasunori Mitsuda and Yoko Shimomura are acclaimed composers known for their work on numerous iconic video game soundtracks. ConcernedApe and Re-Logic are best known as game developers who also contributed music to their games, while Lorien Testard is recognized for his atmospheric and emotional compositions in Clair Obscur: Expedition 33.

songs

My top songs are “Human Sadness” by The Voidz, “Self Destructor,” “Passenger,” “Sunburn,” “Ravaged Rustworks” from Pikmin, and “Stone Wave Cliff” by Lorien Testard. These songs each give off a different vibe, from “Human Sadness” feeling emotional, chaotic, and almost overwhelming, to “Self Destructor” giving a darker, aggressive, and energetic feeling. “Passenger” has a more dreamy and laid-back vibe, while “Sunburn” feels warm, nostalgic, and slightly melancholic. “Ravaged Rustworks” gives off an adventurous, mysterious, and industrial atmosphere, while \ “Stone Wave Cliff” feels peaceful, cinematic, and otherworldly..
